Cookies and storage

Last updated October 24, 2026

Short version: FinanceHub sets no cookies and uses no trackers. It saves a few items in your browser's local storage so the site works, like keeping your progress. Because these are needed for the site to work and aren't used for tracking, we tell you about them instead of asking for consent.

What we save in your browser

NameWhat it's forHow long
financehub-save-v1Your progress, XP, badges, game results and character numbersUntil you clear it
fh-themeRemembers light or dark modeUntil you clear it
fh-langRemembers whether you want the site in English or SpanishUntil you clear it
fh-notice-okRemembers that you closed the storage noticeUntil you clear it
fh-reflect-…Your private reflection answersUntil you clear it
sb-…-auth-tokenKeeps you signed in (only if you have an account). Set by our account provider, Supabase.Until you sign out

What we don't use

No advertising cookies, no analytics, no social media pixels, no fingerprinting, and no third-party font or audio services. If that ever changes, we'll ask for your consent first, with "no" as easy to pick as "yes".

Clearing it

Use the erase button on the privacy page, or clear site data in your browser settings.
