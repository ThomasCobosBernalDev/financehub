About FinanceHub

FinanceHub teaches personal finance and business as one continuous story, from a first paycheck to a Wall Street deal. Every lesson works for different kinds of learners: you can see it, hear it, play it, work out the logic, connect it to nature, read and write about it, talk it through with friends, or think it over on your own.

It's built for people who find normal classes hard to learn from and want to understand what they're doing, why it matters, how it works and who does it for a living.
