About FinancedHub

Free finance lessons, from your first bank account to Wall Street.

We teach money the way it actually works, from your first bank account all the way to a Wall Street deal. Free, in English and Spanish, and taught eight different ways so it sticks.

Why we built FinancedHub

Nobody hands you a manual for money. School gives you formulas. Your first job gives you a W-4 and no explanation. Then one day you're expected to know how credit scores, 401(k)s and stock markets work, and how the people on Wall Street got where they are.

We built FinancedHub to close that gap. It started with one student heading into college finance who wanted more than memorized answers. He wanted to know what to do, why it works, how it works and who does it for a living. When no course did all of that, we built one.

Every lesson connects to the next, so personal finance, economics, business and Wall Street read as one story instead of separate classes. Every fee, tax bracket and exam price is checked against official sources. And it stays free, because knowing how money works shouldn't depend on how much of it you already have.

Our logo

Our logo is a coin cut like a tree trunk. Every year a tree grows, it adds a new ring around everything it already has. Money left to grow works the same way. With compound interest, each year's growth is added on top of all the growth that came before, so every ring wraps around a bigger trunk than the last.

That's what we want for you: knowledge that builds year after year, ring by ring, until it's worth far more than where you started. The rings sit off-center on purpose. No real tree grows perfectly even, and neither does anyone's path with money. What counts is that you keep adding rings.

What we stand for

  • Free, for good. No ads, no paid tiers, no sponsored content.
  • Real numbers. Prices, fees and rules come from the people who set them, with the date we checked.
  • Eight ways to learn. See it, hear it, play it, break it down, link it to nature, write it, talk it through, or think it over alone.
  • Built to get you hired. The course runs all the way to certifications, interviews and the jobs themselves.
