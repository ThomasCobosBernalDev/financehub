Common questions

Getting started

What is FinanceHub?

A free course that teaches money from zero: your first bank account, budgeting, credit and investing, then economics, business and Wall Street, all the way to finance job interviews. It's split into 5 parts and 18 units.

Do I need to know anything first?

No. Part 1 starts with how a checking and savings account works. If you already know the basics, the quick check in setup lets you skip what you know.

Is it really free?

Yes. No ads, no paid tier, no marketing emails.

Where should I start?

Tap Build my path on the home page. Pick why you're learning and you get an ordered list of only the lessons you need.

Your account

Do I need an account?

No. Your progress saves in this browser without one. An account keeps it safe if you clear your browser and lets you pick up on any device.

I forgot my password.

On the sign in page, choose "Forgot your password?" and we'll email you a reset link.

How do I change my username or password?

Open the menu under your name at the top right and choose Settings.

I didn't get the confirmation email.

Check your spam folder, then use "Resend the email" on the sign up screen. It can take a minute to arrive.

Lessons

Why is every lesson taught 8 ways?

People learn differently. Each lesson has parts for seeing, listening, doing, working out the logic, linking to nature, reading and writing, talking it through and thinking on your own.

Can I hide the parts I don't need?

Yes. In Settings under Learning, pick how you learn best. Your picks open first and the rest fold away until you want them.

Some lessons say "soon". When are they coming?

New lessons are added a few at a time, in course order. Everything marked soon is already planned.

How do I find a lesson?

Use Lessons at the top of any page to jump to any part, unit or lesson.

Your path and progress

What is my path?

The lessons picked for your goal, in order. Lessons for things you already know are marked so you can skip them.

How do I change my path?

Go to Settings and choose "Change my path", or retake the setup.

How do XP and badges work?

You earn XP for finishing sections, quizzes and games, and badges for milestones like finishing a lesson.

Troubleshooting

A page looks broken or unstyled.

Refresh the page. If that doesn't fix it, clear this site's cache, or try a private window.

Charts move too much for me.

Turn on "Reduce motion and animations" in Settings. Charts will appear finished instead of drawing in.

The narration won't play.

Check that your device isn't muted and tap play again. Some browsers block sound until you tap the page once.

Privacy and data

What do you save about me?

Your email, username and lesson progress. No tracking or ad cookies. The details are in the privacy policy.

How do I delete my account?

In Settings, scroll to Delete account. It permanently removes your account and the progress saved to it.

How do I erase progress saved in this browser?

Follow the steps on the privacy page.
